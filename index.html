    Our Story

    Beautiful Beginnings, Beautifully Told.

    The story behind Zilovia Babies — a boutique built for beautiful moments.

    Zilovia Babies began with a simple belief — that childhood deserves beautiful things.

    Every piece in our boutique is carefully selected from trusted makers around the world. From soft, timeless everyday essentials to occasion outfits that mark life's sweetest moments, we look for pieces made with comfort, care and beautiful finishing.

    What We Stand For

    We source thoughtfully from carefully chosen makers in the UK, China, Turkey and beyond. We look for pieces that feel as beautiful as they look — soft fabrics, gentle finishing, and designs that make everyday moments feel a little more special.

    Made for Nigerian Mums

    We understand what modern Nigerian mothers want — beautiful pieces at prices that make sense, delivered quickly across Lagos and nationwide, packaged with the care of a gift. Every order is packed by hand with tissue paper, a ribbon and a small note.

    Beautiful Beginnings

    Answered

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything you need to know about shopping with Zilovia Babies.

    How do I place an order?
    Browse our shop, add pieces you love to your cart, and check out via WhatsApp for the fastest response. You can also send us a DM on Instagram or use the contact form on our contact page.
    What size should I choose for my baby?
    We recommend using our size guide (available on every product page) based on your baby's age, weight and height. When in doubt, size up — babies grow quickly, and a little room lets the piece last longer.
    Do you deliver nationwide?
    Yes — we deliver anywhere in Nigeria. Lagos deliveries are typically same or next day. Abuja, Port Harcourt and other major cities take 2–3 days. Other states typically take 3–5 days.
    What payment methods do you accept?
    We accept bank transfers, card payments, and payment on delivery within Lagos. Payment details are shared once your order is confirmed on WhatsApp.
    Where do your pieces come from?
    We carefully source from trusted makers in the UK, China, Turkey and beyond, choosing pieces that meet our standards for comfort, quality and beautiful finishing.
    Can I return or exchange an item?
    Yes — see our returns page for full details. Items must be unworn, unwashed and in their original packaging, and returned within 7 days of delivery.
    Do you offer gift wrapping?
    Every order is beautifully packaged with tissue paper and ribbon at no extra charge — because every Zilovia piece should feel like a gift. Let us know if it's for a specific occasion and we'll add a handwritten note.
    How can I stay updated on new arrivals?
    Follow us on Instagram @ziloviababies for weekly new-arrival previews, styling inspiration and early access to sales. You can also DM us to join our WhatsApp broadcast list.
    Shipping

    Delivery Information

    Fast, careful delivery to your doorstep, wherever you are in Nigeria.

    Delivery Timelines

    • Lagos (Mainland & Island): Same day or next day
    • Abuja, Port Harcourt, Ibadan: 2–3 business days
    • Other states: 3–5 business days
    • International: Available on request — please contact us

    Delivery Fees

    Delivery fees vary by location and are calculated at checkout. Lagos deliveries typically range from ₦1,500 – ₦3,000. Interstate deliveries range from ₦3,000 – ₦6,000 depending on state and package weight.

    Order Processing

    Orders placed before 2pm are processed the same day. Orders placed after 2pm or on weekends are processed the next business day.

    Packaging

    Every order is carefully hand-packed with tissue paper, ribbon and a note — arriving looking like the gift it deserves to be.

    Tracking Your Order

    Once your order is dispatched, we'll send you a tracking number via WhatsApp or SMS. You can reach out to us anytime for updates.

    Returns & Exchanges

    Our Returns Policy

    We want you to love every piece. Here's how returns and exchanges work.

    Our Promise

    If a piece isn't quite right — the wrong size, not what you expected — you can return or exchange it within 7 days of delivery.

    Return Conditions

    • Items must be unworn, unwashed and in original condition
    • All original tags must be attached
    • Items must be in their original packaging
    • Returns must be initiated within 7 days of delivery

    Non-Returnable Items

    • Underwear, socks and hair accessories (for hygiene)
    • Sale or clearance items
    • Personalised or custom items

    How to Return

    Contact us via WhatsApp or email with your order number and reason for return. We'll guide you through the process and arrange collection or provide a return address.

    Refunds

    Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we'll process your refund within 3–5 business days to your original payment method. Exchanges are subject to stock availability.

    Damaged or Incorrect Items

    If your item arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We'll make it right immediately.
